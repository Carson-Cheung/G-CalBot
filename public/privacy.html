Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

G-CalBot is a free Discord bot and Discord Activity that sends you reminders for your calendar events. This page explains what it stores, why, who else is involved, and how to delete your data. G-CalBot is a hobby project run by one developer; it has no ads, does not sell data, and does not use AI.

What we store

Data Why
Your Discord user ID To know whose calendars and settings are whose, and where to send reminders.
The calendar links you add (secret iCal addresses) and the names you give them To read your upcoming events.
Your settings: reminder lead time, time zone, daily summary on/off and time To send reminders and summaries the way you asked.
Records of reminders already sent (your user ID, an event ID and the lead time) So you don't get the same reminder twice. Deleted automatically after 7 days.

Calendar events are not stored. The bot fetches your feeds every few minutes and keeps the events in memory only, to check for upcoming reminders and to show your schedule. They are gone when the bot restarts.

We never see your Google password or email login, and we don't use Google sign-in. A secret iCal address gives read-only access to one calendar; anyone who has it can read that calendar, so we keep it private and never show it to other users.

What other people can see

Most commands reply only to you. When you use a public command like /cal share, the event titles, times and locations you choose to share are posted in that channel. Your calendar links and email are never included.

The web dashboard

When you open the dashboard inside Discord, Discord confirms who you are and we create a signed login token containing your Discord user ID, display name and avatar link. It is saved in your browser's local storage and expires after 7 days. We don't use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts.

Services we rely on

Service What it does with your data
Discord Delivers commands, messages and DMs between you and the bot.
Render Hosts the bot. Server logs can include Discord user IDs and error details, and are kept by Render for a limited time.
Neon Hosts the database that holds the data listed above.
Google Fonts The dashboard loads its font from Google, which receives your IP address when the page loads.
Your calendar provider (e.g. Google) The bot requests your feed from the address you gave it, as any calendar app would.

How long we keep it

Your data stays until you remove it. Sent-reminder records are deleted after 7 days. Calendar events are never stored.

Deleting your data

  • /cal unlink removes one calendar, or all of them.
  • /cal deletedata permanently deletes everything we store about you: calendars, links, settings and reminder records. Reminders stop immediately.

You can also ask for a copy of your data, or for it to be deleted, by opening an issue on GitHub.

Security

Connections use HTTPS, feed requests are limited to public addresses, and every dashboard request is checked against your login. No system is perfectly secure; if you think your secret link was exposed, reset it in Google Calendar settings (Integrate calendar → Reset) and link the new one.

Children

G-CalBot is for Discord users, who must be at least 13 (or older where local law requires).

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too. Significant changes will be announced in the bot's /comms list or on GitHub.
