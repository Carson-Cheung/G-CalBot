🔒 Privacy Protected: We use your Google Calendar's private iCal link. Your email address and secret feed URLs are never broadcasted to other Discord members, and all updates sync automatically.

⚠️ Disclaimer: Data contained within your calendar entries (such as event titles, descriptions, and locations) can be visible in Discord reminders and embeds—please keep this in mind when using public commands like /cal share.

ℹ️ Where to find your Secret iCal link in Google Calendar
  1. Open calendar.google.com on your browser.
  2. On the left sidebar, hover over your calendar, click the three dots (⋮), and choose Settings and sharing.
  3. Scroll down to the "Integrate calendar" section.
  4. Find "Secret address in iCal format" and click Copy (Google may show a security prompt—this is normal).
  5. Paste the copied URL below!
