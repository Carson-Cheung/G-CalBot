ℹ️ Where to find your Secret iCal link
  1. Open calendar.google.com in your browser.
  2. On the left sidebar, hover over your calendar, click the three dots (⋮), and choose Settings and sharing.
  3. Scroll down to the "Integrate calendar" section.
  4. Find "Secret address in iCal format" and click Copy (Google may show a security notice—this is normal).
  5. Paste the copied URL above!

🔒 Privacy Protected: We use your Google Calendar's private iCal read-only feed. Your Google credentials and passwords are never accessed or stored.

⚠️ Notice: Event titles, descriptions, and locations will be delivered via Discord direct messages and shown in bot embeds.
